# Example Organization: Records and Requests Policy

## Purpose

This fictional policy demonstrates the local retrieval pipeline. It is not based on any employer, institution, or real operating policy.

## Request approval

Employees must submit a written request to their team lead before purchasing equipment with organizational funds. The request should identify the item, estimated cost, business purpose, and requested purchase date.

A team lead may approve requests up to $500. Requests above $500 require approval from both the team lead and the finance coordinator before the purchase is made.

## Record retention

Approved requests, receipts, and related correspondence should be retained for three years after the end of the fiscal year in which the purchase occurred.

## Exceptions

Emergency purchases may be made before written approval when delaying the purchase would create an immediate safety risk or prevent essential operations. The employee must document the reason and request retroactive approval within two business days.
